## About

Hello, I'm Igor. I'm building **{{ app.name }}**, a personal medical
information manager, and writing about the process in public — the decisions,
the dead ends, and the parts I get wrong.

The blog is mostly a build log, with room for the other things that interest
me: software, medicine, privacy, and the occasional story. Some of what I write
will be technical, most of it will not need to be.

If you want to follow along, start with the [latest posts](/blog/), or read about
[the software projects](/software/).
