15 Glyph Outlines ¶

niori-pdf-glyphs.el extracts the outline contours of a glyph from a TrueType (glyf) or CFF/OpenType (CharStrings) font and draws them with path primitives. This is the port of cl-pdf’s glyph rendering; it is handy for watermark-style decorative lettering at any size.

Function: niori-pdf-draw-glyph-outline font-file char x y size &key fill stroke line-width ¶

Draw the outline of char from font-file (a .ttf or .otf path) with its baseline at (x, y) scaled to size points. fill/stroke are RGB lists or nil.
