10 Outlines (Bookmarks) ¶

niori-pdf-outlines.el builds the PDF outline tree (the bookmark panel). Flow headings record outline entries automatically; for raw documents add entries with niori-pdf-outlines-add.

Function: niori-pdf-outlines-add doc title page &key level y ¶

Add an outline entry title pointing to page (1-based) of doc. level (default 1) nests under the previous entry of the nearest shallower level; y is the vertical destination in PDF coordinates (nil = page top).

Function: niori-pdf-outlines-count doc ¶

Number of accumulated entries.

Function: niori-pdf-outlines-clear doc ¶

Forget all entries of doc.

The tree is written during niori-pdf-save; the catalog receives /Outlines and /PageMode /UseOutlines. A document with outline entries only (no flow TOC) works fine as well.
