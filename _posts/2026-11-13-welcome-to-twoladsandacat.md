This site runs on Jekyll with the TeXt theme. New posts go in `_posts/`
using the `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` naming convention - the date in the
filename becomes the publication date. Keep the filename prefix in the
past (or omit `date:` in the front matter), because Jekyll does not
publish future-dated posts.

Tip: do not set `layout:` in a post's front matter unless you know the
theme's layout names - the site default (`article` for posts) already
applies from `_config.yml`.
