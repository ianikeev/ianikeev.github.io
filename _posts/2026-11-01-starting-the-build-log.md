I'm building a medical information manager — a place to keep a person's own
medical history together, in a form they control — and I've decided to do it in
public. This is the first entry.

<!--more-->

The reason is not marketing. It's that the interesting parts of this project are
the decisions, and decisions are easier to make well when they're written down
somewhere I can be held to them later. Writing in public also means I can't
quietly revise my reasoning six months from now and pretend it was always the
plan.

There is nothing to download yet. No waitlist, no sign-up. The [What I'm
building](/building/) page says where the project is; this blog says how it's
going.

## What to expect here

- **Build notes.** Choices about the data model, import formats, and how much
  the app is allowed to guess about your records.
- **Dead ends.** Things I tried and abandoned, and what made me abandon them.
- **Occasional tangents.** Medicine, privacy, software, and whatever else I'm
  reading.

Some posts will be technical. Most won't need to be. If you only ever read one
kind, read the second.
